Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my files stored?
Margin.alia stores all your documents as plain .md files in whatever folder you choose on your Mac. There's no cloud storage and no proprietary database — your files are always on your disk, in standard Markdown format.
What happens to my files if I delete Margin.alia?
Nothing. Your .md files remain exactly where they are on your Mac. They're plain text files that any Markdown editor or text editor can open.
How do pasteboards work?
Pasteboards let you pin notes, PDFs, screenshots, and images alongside a document — without modifying the document file itself. Pasteboard data is stored separately in the app's support folder.
How do tags work?
Margin.alia uses macOS's native file tagging system. Tags you add in the app appear in Finder, and tags you've already applied in Finder show up in Margin.alia automatically.
What export formats are supported?
Margin.alia exports to PDF and Word (.docx). Use Publish mode to choose a style preset, customize typography and layout, and export with a single click.
