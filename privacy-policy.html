Privacy Policy

Alex  ·  Last updated: March 24, 2026

Alex is a macOS writing application developed by Scott Cunningham. This privacy policy explains what information Alex does and does not collect.

No Data Collection

Alex does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data or usage information. Specifically:

  • Alex does not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers.
  • Alex does not track how you use the app or which features you access.
  • Alex does not send any data to external servers or third-party services.
  • Alex does not use analytics, crash reporting services, or advertising SDKs.

Your Files Stay on Your Device

All documents you create or edit in Alex are stored as plain .md files in folders you choose on your Mac. They never leave your device unless you explicitly share them. Alex has no access to your files beyond what you grant through macOS file permissions.

macOS File Tags

Alex integrates with macOS native file tagging (Finder tags). Tags are stored as standard macOS extended attributes, managed entirely by your operating system. No tag data is transmitted anywhere.

No Third-Party Services

Alex does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services. There are no embedded SDKs that collect information about you or your device.

Children's Privacy

Because Alex does not collect any data from any users, it presents no special risks with respect to children's privacy.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date.
